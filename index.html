The kit

Eight voices, set up for dub techno. The kick is LXFM-1's own; every other voice is an 808 or 909 sound. Each voice is mixed to a fixed place under the kick, so turning knobs or randomising never throws the mix off. That makes it safe to play live.

Effects ready at power-on

A dotted-1/8 dub delay with long, dark feedback; a big spring reverb; a little swing. The kick ducks the chord, ride and open hat. The kick stays dry while the chord goes heavily to the delay.

Patterns that stay in style

The generator writes dub techno. The kick holds four on the floor and snare and clap land once a bar on 2 or 4. Hats sit off the beat and come and go, and chord stabs fall between the beats so the delay fills the space.

Levels you can trust

With master at maximum a kick peaks around −14.5 dBFS. A limiter and soft clip keep even a deliberate worst case under full scale.

No menu diving

Black keys pick the voice, white keys are its 16 steps. Every voice's key flashes on its hits, the selected one stays lit, and a muted voice's key blinks fast.

OCT− and OCT+

OCT− gives the selected voice a new sound. OCT+ gives it a new dub pattern.

MUTATE

Small changes to every voice except the kick: knobs move a little, and one or two steps change. Backbeats and downbeats stay.

RANDOM ALL

New sounds and new patterns for every voice at once.

Steps

Up to 64 steps per voice. Hold a step and turn a knob to set probability, ratchet, accent and nudge. Tap the selected voice's key again for its next step page.

99 patterns

Tap SAVE, choose 01 to 99, then SAVE, LOAD or DELETE. Saved slots are filled and empty ones outlined. A pattern holds everything: sounds, steps, tempo and effects, so there is no separate kit to save. Loading works while it plays, and power-on loads the pattern you used last. A power cut during a save can't damage the other patterns.

Sequence other gear

In GLO > SYSTEM, the MOUT setting sends every step as a MIDI note over USB: BOTH plays the FM-1 and sends; SEQ makes the FM-1 a silent sequencer. Notes follow the Vermona DRM1 MKIV's order and default notes on channel 10, so it plays a DRM1 straight away. Accented steps send velocity 127, others 96.

The FM-1 and most drum machines both have device-type USB ports, so put a USB MIDI host box between them (for example a Kenton MIDI USB Host or a Retrokits RK-006) and run a DIN cable to the drum machine's MIDI IN.

LXFM-1 Control for Android

All eight voices' steps on one screen, mutes, play and tempo, and dice buttons for new beats and sounds, per voice or for all. Connect the phone to the FM-1 with a USB cable. Android 6.0 or newer.

Install

Flash from the browser

The easiest way: open the flasher page in Chrome or Edge on a computer, connect the FM-1 by USB and press Install. Or with M-Flasher+:

  1. Download LXFM-1-1.4.fwsc.
  2. Connect the FM-1 to your computer by USB.
  3. Open M-Flasher+ and flash the file, the same way as an official update.
  4. The FM-1 restarts with the LXFM-1 screen. To go back, flash M-VAVE's official firmware the same way.

Install at your own risk. LXFM-1 is a community firmware, not made or supported by M-VAVE. Uploading your own samples is not available: the 99 patterns use that memory.
